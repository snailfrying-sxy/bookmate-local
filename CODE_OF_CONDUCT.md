# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

We expect contributors and maintainers to communicate with respect, focus on
the work, welcome good-faith questions, and protect the privacy of readers and
their data. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, and sharing
private books, notes, conversations, credentials, or security details without
permission are not acceptable.

## Scope

This applies to project discussions, issues, pull requests, releases, and
other spaces representing BookMate Local.

## Enforcement

Before a public issue tracker and maintainer contact are established, reports
should be kept private. Maintainers may remove content, reject contributions,
or suspend participation when this standard is violated. Enforcement decisions
should record relevant evidence and avoid unnecessary disclosure of private data.
