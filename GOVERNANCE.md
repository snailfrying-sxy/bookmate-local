# Governance

## Maintainer-Led Project

BookMate Local is maintained through a maintainer-led model. Maintainers decide
on releases, architecture, security fixes, compatibility policy, and acceptance
of contributions after considering user privacy, local-first operation, and the
project's noncommercial license.

## Decision Records

Material product and architecture decisions belong in `docs/`. Changes that
affect storage schemas, model-data disclosure, licensing, or public contracts
must include a short decision record or an update to the relevant specification.

## Contributions and Releases

Contributions are reviewed for correctness, tests, security, privacy, and
license compatibility. A release must record its version, migration notes,
security impact, and verification results. Commercial licensing decisions are
outside the normal contribution process and require the copyright holder.
